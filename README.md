### Cristian Aguilar

Doctoral candidate in Design at Universidad de Palermo (Buenos Aires) and university lecturer in Chile (UAI, USM, UNAB). I work with AI agents (Claude Code) both in research and in the tools I build for teaching.

**Research.** Fifteen years of websites from startups funded by Start-Up Chile (2010–2025), rebuilt from the Internet Archive: 1,268 startups and 7,337 startup-year pages, each traceable to its snapshot. Current question: on whose platforms do state-funded startups build, and how locked in do they become?
Code: [startup-web-archive-pipeline](https://github.com/agncris/startup-web-archive-pipeline).

**Teaching tools.** [NeumaLab](https://github.com/agncris/Mec275-Neumatica): a virtual lab for pneumatics, PLC, CNC and robotics used by my MEC275 students ([live](https://mec275.vercel.app)). I also designed and teach *Innovation and Digital Strategy in the New Economy* (UAI), on platform economics.

**Practice.** [FUTUROX](https://futurox.cl), strategic innovation and futures thinking.
